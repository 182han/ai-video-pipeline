## 6. 调色与质感

- 通用：`cinematic color grading` + `fine 35mm film grain` + `natural contrast`
- 高光控制（必写）：`controlled highlights, no blown-out sky`
- 冷：`cold cyan shadows, warm highlights`（压饱和 teal-orange）
- 空气：`subtle atmospheric haze, layered depth through haze`
- 单色：`near-monochrome palette, one dominant hue`
- 暖浓：`rich warm palette, ochre and rust, cool shadow separation`
- 星云：`deep dark base, luminous warm nebula, magenta and teal`
